How to use this

Log it while the credits roll

The useful reaction lasts about ten minutes after a film ends. Open the film, tap Watched, tap a rating, and write two lines. That's the whole ritual — under two minutes.

The chapters are a sequence, not a ranking. Each one sets up the next. But if a chapter isn't where your head is right now, skip it and come back — the order is a suggestion, not a rule.
